# Ramon Llull and the *Ars Magna*

> *Ramon Llull (Mallorca, 1232 – 1316) — combinatorial rotating wheels of universal concepts.*

Llull's *Ars Magna* (Great Art) is the earliest European prototype of a **geometric reasoning system**: rotating concentric wheels with letters representing universal concepts (Goodness, Greatness, Eternity, Power, Wisdom, Will, Virtue, Truth, Glory, Difference, Concordance, Contrariety…) that, by rotation, generate all possible combinations of subjects, predicates, and qualifiers.

The *Ars* was not a metaphor — it was a **functional system** for systematic reasoning, intended to reach demonstrable conclusions in theology, philosophy, law, and inter-religious dialogue. It is the direct ancestor of:

- **Combinatorial logic** (Leibniz explicitly cited Llull as inspiration for his *characteristica universalis*)
- **Symbolic computing** (Eco, Gardner, and others have traced the lineage to modern formal systems)
- **Geometric ontology** (the wheels are 2D circular abstractions; the Meta-Globàlium extends to 3D and 4D hyperspheres)

## Why Llull matters for the Meta-Globàlium

Three contributions:

### 1. Geometric form for unified reasoning

Llull's wheels are *circles* — the simplest of perfect figures. By rotation, every concept enters relation with every other. The Meta-Globàlium extends this insight: from circle to sphere, from sphere to hypersphere. The principle is the same — geometric symmetry as the substrate of unified reasoning.

### 2. Universal concepts as combinatorial primitives

Llull's nine universal *Dignities* (Goodness, Greatness, Eternity, Power, Wisdom, Will, Virtue, Truth, Glory) and nine *Relative principles* (Difference, Concordance, Contrariety, Beginning, Middle, End, Majority, Equality, Minority) function as **structural primitives** — irreducible terms whose combinations generate the space of meaningful claims.

The Meta-Globàlium does not adopt Llull's specific nines (the Dignities are theologically loaded). It adopts the *form*: a small set of axes, irreducible to each other, generating a structured space by combination.

### 3. Inter-tradition dialogue

Llull explicitly designed the *Ars* to enable **structured dialogue** between Christian, Muslim, and Jewish thinkers — a culturally neutral framework on top of which differences could be articulated. The Meta-Globàlium inherits this aspiration: a structural form general enough to host different axiological commitments, on top of which traditions can disagree in articulate ways.

## What Llull did *not* do

For honesty:

- The wheels are 2D, not 3D. The sphere as model of thought is not yet there.
- The combinatorics is Aristotelian, not yet dialectical. Tensions of opposites are not the primary structuring move.
- The Dignities are theologically substantive (not value-neutral). The *Ars* is Christian-anchored.

The lineage from Llull to the Meta-Globàlium is one of **inheritance with modification**. The combinatorial impulse and the geometric form are taken; the specific terms and the religious anchoring are not.
