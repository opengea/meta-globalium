# Canonical mappings of the Meta-Globàlium

> **Provenance**: this content is part of the **Meta-Globàlium proper** — the new structural layer (Berenguer / Opengea, 2023–2026) added on top of the inherited Globàlium ontology.

> **Principle**: the Neutral cube of the Globàlium has eight vertices with canonical mathematical operations. These operations have **systematic correspondences** with Boolean logic, set theory, types of inference, Aristotelian causes, and communicative pedagogy. This document consolidates all those correspondences.

## 1. Mathematical operations + logic gates + set theory (cube vertices)

Each vertex of the Neutral cube has three formal correspondences that coincide structurally:

| Category | Mathematical operation | Family | Logic gate | Set-theoretic operation |
|----------|-----------------------|--------|-----------|-------------------------|
| **NOU** | identity (=) | unifying | **BUFFER** | empty set ∅ + universal U |
| **OBJ** | distinction | dividing | **NOT** | partition {{1,2},{3}} |
| **MTP** | convergence (∩, ≅, →) | unifying | **AND** | power set P(A) |
| **SUB** | correspondence (↔, ≡) | unifying | **OR** | Cartesian product A × B |
| **MTF** | similarity (~) | unifying | **XNOR** *(implicit)* | inclusion A ⊆ B |
| **CIE** | difference (−, ⊖) | dividing | **XOR** | set difference A − B |
| **ART** | divergence (∇⋅, ∨) | dividing | **NAND** *(universal)* | symmetric difference A Δ B |
| **FEN** | polarity (±) | dividing | **NOR** *(universal)* | (under exploration) |

### 1.1 Significance of "universal" in Boolean logic

**NAND** and **NOR** are *universal* gates: any Boolean function can be built using only one of them. This gives **ART (NAND)** and **FEN (NOR)** a special operational status — they are the **fundamental generative operations** of the Boolean system, equivalent to the inverse claim "everything can be synthesised from divergence or polarity".

### 1.2 Cube neighbourhoods via logic gates

- **Coincidence principle**: AND ↔ XNOR (MTP ↔ MTF) — unifying operations
- **Non-coincidence principle**: NAND ↔ XOR (ART ↔ CIE) — dividing operations
- BUFFER (NOU) and NOT (OBJ) are the unary operations — the most fundamental cardinal poles

## 2. System of causes (Aristotle ↔ Globàlium)

The four Aristotelian causes + three modern extensions map systematically onto specific neutrals:

| Aristotelian cause | Globàlium category | Sense |
|--------------------|--------------------|-------|
| **Material** cause | OBJ (Identity) | what it is made of — substance, *hylé*, *res extensa* |
| **Formal** cause | OBJ (Identity) | what form it has — *eidos*, configuration |
| **Efficient** cause | SUB (Alterity) | who or what produces it — agent, *res cogitans* |
| **Final** cause | OBJ (Identity) | what it is for — *telos*, finality |
| **Exemplary** cause | DIV (Mundane MIT) | the transcendent model that orients — divine model |
| **Conditional** / **contextual** cause | FEN (Multiplicity) | the circumstances — context, situation |
| **Essential** cause | ORG (Plasma MTF) | the underlying vital force — *orgone*, substrate |

### 2.1 Implication for the verifier

The structural verifier can evaluate the **causal coverage** of a response: an explanation that only covers material/formal cause (OBJ) without touching final cause (OBJ-finality), efficient (SUB), or contextual (FEN) is **causally incomplete**. Analogous to the dispersed coverage of principles.

## 3. Three types of inference ↔ neutrals

| Inference | Category | Logical direction | Domain |
|-----------|---------|-------------------|--------|
| **Deduction** | LOG (Formality) | general → particular | formal logic, mathematics |
| **Induction** | IDE (Regulability) | particular → general | empirical sciences, law |
| **Selective abduction** | EST (Informality) | toward the best explanation *(known options)* | diagnosis, aesthetics, hermeneutics |
| **Creative abduction** | MIT (Orientability) | generation of new hypotheses | scientific innovation, art, myths |

### 3.1 Implication for the verifier

The verifier can measure **epistemological coverage**: a purely deductive (LOG) response without induction (IDE) or abduction (EST/MIT) is epistemologically partial. A complete answer should integrate all four kinds of inference.

### 3.2 Links to the Method cycle

- ANA (analysis) works with deduction (toward particulars)
- SIN (synthesis) works with induction (toward generals)
- AMO + EXP integrate abduction (as practical option)

## 5. Tempeternitat — D4 as transversal meta-dimension *(adopted 2026-05-02)*

> **Status**: confirmed 2026-05-02. The D4 dimension (radial PLA-MON) of the Meta-Globàlium is canonically called **tempeternitat** (Catalan portmanteau: *temps* + *eternitat*). It is not the "fourth dimension" in the sense of a Cartesian addition — it is the **transversal meta-dimension** that explains why every cube pole has 3 layers (Plasmàtica/Neutral/Mundana).

### 5.1 Canonical definition

**Tempeternitat**: radial continuum between absolute atemporality (PLA, pre-temporal seed) and full manifest temporality (MON, unfolded cosmos). In its unfolding it contains all possible modes of time.

| Tempeternal state | Anchor / layer |
|-------------------|----------------|
| Absolute atemporality | PLA (radial centre) |
| Presence ('being' constant) | intermediate plasmatic layer |
| Simultaneity (all-at-once) | balanced plasmatic layer |
| Operational equilibrium (effective present) | Neutral layer |
| Cyclical eternity (infinite succession) | intermediate mundane layer |
| Linear time (historicity) | balanced mundane layer |
| Absolute full temporality | MON (surface) |

The name **tempeternitat** explicitly rejects the classical time-eternity duality and converts it into a continuous gradient — a philosophical move that distinguishes the Globàlium from other cosmovisions.

### 5.2 Transversality — operates on every pole

Tempeternitat is **not another axis alongside the others**: it is the unfolding principle that operates **on every Cartesian pole**. Each cube pole has its three tempeternal moments that constitute its canonical PLA-NEU-MON triad.

For each Cartesian pole, the canonical PLA / NEU / MON triad is documented in [`../ontology/data/categories.json`](../ontology/data/categories.json). 26 poles × 3 tempeternal moments = 78 + 2 anchorings (PLA, MON) = 80 canonical categories.

### 5.3 PLA and MON as absolute horizons

PLA and MON **are not categories of their own** but **limit states** of tempeternitat applied to the whole cube:

- PLA (centre) = tempeternitat-zero = absolute atemporality of all poles simultaneously
- MON (surface) = tempeternitat-full = complete temporal manifestation of all poles simultaneously

That is why they are **anchorings**, not categories of the count.

### 5.4 Implications for knowledge (3 moments)

Deep knowledge **integrates the three tempeternal moments** of every concept:

| Moment | Aspect | Risk if missing |
|--------|--------|-----------------|
| PLA-side | essential principles, universal seeds, the atemporal | "factual without depth of principle" |
| NEU | current dialectical balance, effective present, operational equilibrium | "abstract-eternal without grounding" or "factual without centre" |
| MON-side | practical application, concrete facts, manifest historical unfolding | "abstract-eternal without grounding" |

Wisdom **integrates the three**. The structural verifier can measure this integration with a `tempeternal_coverage` metric.

### 5.5 Connection to Solve-Coagula

The recursive iteration of the Method (Solve-Coagula meta-operation) **operates along the tempeternal radial**:

- **Solve** (ANA + CIE) = movement toward PLA = re-dissolution toward the atemporal essential seed
- **Coagula** (SIN + MTP) = movement toward MON = re-coagulation toward temporal manifestation

Each iteration traverses the tempeternal axis in both directions, alternating return-to-the-essential with re-application-in-manifestation.

### 5.6 Integrated philosophical support

- **David Bohm — Holomovement**: implicate order (folded, atemporal) ↔ explicate order (unfolded, temporal) = exactly the PLA-MON tempeternal continuum
- **Schopenhauer**: the Will (atemporal) lives at the PLA horizon; the world as representation lives toward MON
- **Stephen Wolfram — Ruliad**: "all time unfolded" = MON; "everything has already happened" = PLA (eternal tempeternal perspective)
- **Global mystical traditions** (Vedanta, Christian mysticism, Sufism, Zen) all articulate a time-eternity continuum — the Globàlium gives it a proper canonical name

## 6. Solve-Coagula as META-OPERATION of the Method

> **Status**: adopted 2026-05-02. Solve-Coagula is NOT a 7th structural element but the **iterating meta-operation** that recursively applies the Method cycle. **Directional definition**: Solve-Coagula IS the FEN→NOU movement (essentialization).

### 6.1 Fundamental directional definition

**Solve-Coagula IS literally the FEN→NOU movement**:

- **Solve** = descending analysis (FEN→TEO): dissolve the phenomenon into theory
- **Coagula** = ascending synthesis (TEO→NOU): coagulate theory into essence
- → The complete FEN→NOU movement (via TEO) **is** Solve-Coagula. Without this journey, the operation does not exist.

**Critical consequence**: Solve-Coagula is not a universal property of the cube — it is a **directional operation**. It applies only to responses/operations that essentialize (move from FEN toward NOU). For orientation or existential localization (which have the inverse NOU→FEN movement), Solve-Coagula **does not apply**.

### 6.2 Applicability by cycle

| Cycle | Trajectory | Includes FEN→NOU? | Solve-Coagula |
|-------|-----------|-------------------|---------------|
| **Method** | FEN→ANA→TEO→SIN→NOU→AMO→PRA→EXP→FEN | ✅ yes, via ANA-SIN | ✅ applicable |
| **Knowledge** | FEN→ART→SUB→MTP→NOU→MTF→OBJ→CIE→FEN | ✅ yes, via 4 face operations (CIE/MTP are direct Solve-Coagula; ART/MTF also essentialize) | ✅ applicable |
| **Relation / Orientation** | NOU→CNF→FEN→CMN→MON→EXC→PLA→ATZ→NOU | ❌ no — inverse NOU→FEN movement (orientation, not essentialization) | ❌ inapplicable |

### 6.3 Architectural distinction between types of canonical units

The Meta-Globàlium architecture distinguishes two types of canonical units:

- **Static elements** (10 in total): 6 axiomatic principles + 4 disciplinary dialectics. They describe *how* the Neutral cube is.
- **Meta-operations** (Solve-Coagula being the first): iterative operations that apply the static elements dynamically. They describe *how the cube is used* to generate knowledge. **Directional**: each meta-operation has a defined movement on the cube and a conditioned applicability.

### 6.4 Iterative formalization

The agent anchored to the Meta-Globàlium applies Solve-Coagula as **recursive iteration** on its responses:

```
Iteration 0: Question → initial response
Iteration 1: Solve the initial response (analyse, criticise, differentiate)
             → Coagula improved response (synthesise, integrate)
Iteration 2: Solve response of iter 1 → Coagula iter 2
…
Iteration N: convergence or stability
```

At each iteration, the structural verifier measures:

- **Static completeness** (𝓗 over the 10 elements + causal + epistemological coverage)
- **Dynamic balance** (`solve_coagula_balance`: 1.0 = perfectly balanced)
- **Convergence between iterations** (improvement of 𝓗 between iter N-1 and N)

The iteration stops when:

- 𝓗 exceeds a threshold (e.g. 0.85)
- or the improvement between iterations falls below a minimum
- or a maximum iteration count is reached

The alchemical aphorism *solve et coagula* (attributed to Basil Valentine, 15th century) inscribes the Meta-Globàlium within the European hermetic tradition: the iterative cycle of conceptual transformation as foundation of deep knowledge.
