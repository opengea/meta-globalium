# Meta-Globàlium

> A geometry of human thought — an open ontological model for anchoring artificial intelligence to a verifiable structure of meaning.

**Meta-Globàlium** is a hyperdimensional ontological model — a *geometry of thought* — that organizes the totality of human reality along four reflective dialectical axes, projected from a 4D hypersphere onto 3D space. It is the structural backbone of **Arkadium**, a neuro-symbolic AI architecture proposed as an alternative to brute scaling.

This repository is the **open commons** of the model: the ontology, the methodology, the lineage, the documentation. It is intended to be adopted, audited, extended, translated, and improved by the international community — across philosophy, AI research, knowledge representation, education, and beyond.

> The implementation (the Arkadium product, the 3D Metamodeler tool) is developed separately. **What lives here is the model itself** — what any architecture can adopt as a structural verifier or knowledge backbone.

## Why this matters

Current AI deficits — opacity, lack of depth, lack of generalization — are not solved by scaling more parameters or piling up ethical rules. They are solved by anchoring AI to a **geometry of thought**: a form where every concept has a place, has opposites, and stands in relations, rather than floating isolated within a statistical cloud.

The Meta-Globàlium proposes a verifier for domains without ground truth — ethics, judgment, value, politics — based not on lists of rules but on a **relational structure** of dialectical axes. **The Good is the geometric property of not collapsing any dimension** — a structural criterion, not an axiological one, and culturally neutral for that reason.

The intuition is not new. From Llull's combinatorial wheels (13th century) to Xirinacs's spherical topology (1997), thinkers have sought a geometric form for unified reasoning. Only now is it computationally operative.

## Two layers: inherited Globàlium + Meta-Globàlium

The repository documents **two layers**, distinguished by provenance:

- **Inherited Globàlium ontology** — the canonical model articulated by Xirinacs (1997, doctoral thesis) and exposed pedagogically by Berenguer (*Globàlium · petit manual*, 2024). The 4 axes, 8 axis poles, 8 central mediators, 12 disciplines, 80 canonical categories, and 6 maximal cycles — these are documentation of an existing tradition.
- **Meta-Globàlium proper** — the new structural layer (Berenguer / Opengea, 2023–2026) added on top: the 6 axiomatic principles + 8 mathematical operations + 4 disciplinary dialectics, the fractal N1/N2 architecture, the *tempeternitat* D4 framing, the Solve-Coagula meta-operation, the structural verifier 𝓗(r), the N2 frame protocol, and the Good-as-harmony criterion.

Each document carries a **Provenance** marker at the top indicating which layer it belongs to.

## Lineage and authorship

The Meta-Globàlium consolidates a centuries-long line of European thought on geometric models of knowledge. The four reflective axes appear, in different formulations, in German idealism, hermeneutics, European phenomenology, and Zen Buddhism. The concrete instantiation in 80 categories comes from the Catalan philosophical lineage:

- **Ramon Llull** (1232–1316), *Ars Magna* — combinatorial rotating wheels of universal concepts
- **Lluís Maria Xirinacs** (1932–2007), *A global model of reality* (1997, doctoral thesis, Univ. of Barcelona) — first explicit spherical / toroidal topology

The current consolidation is by **Jordi Berenguer Rodrigo** (Opengea SCCL, 2023–2026), with contributions from Laura Rubinat-Ripoll and Frederic Monras Vidiella.

The model is **revisable, open to dialogue with other traditions**, and its value is to be judged by effective utility — not by any claim to ultimate truth.
