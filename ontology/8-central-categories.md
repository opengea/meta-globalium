# The 8 central categories (secondary mediators)

> **Provenance**: this content describes the **Globàlium ontology** — inherited from the canonical model articulated by Lluís Maria Xirinacs (1997, *A global model of reality*) and the pedagogical exposition by Jordi Berenguer (*Globàlium · petit manual*, 2024). It is documentation of an existing model, not original work of this repository.


Eight categories sit on the Neutral mediator sphere at generation 1.5 — between the **8 axis poles** (gen 1.0) and the **12 disciplines** (gen 1.75). They are the **central categories** of the Meta-Globàlium: the operational primitives through which the Mètode Global moves, and the joints around which the rest of the structure articulates.

They form **two groups of four**, each describing a circular trajectory (*volta*) across the hypersphere:

| Group | Trajectory | Categories | Role |
|-------|-----------|------------|------|
| **Application Cycle** *(Volta d'Aplicació)* | vertical meridian (x = 0) connecting TEO/PRA × FEN/NOU | ANA · SIN · AMO · EXP | the four phases of the **Mètode Global** — analysis, synthesis, love, experience |
| **Revelation Cycle** *(Volta de Revelació)* | lateral meridian (z = 0) connecting SUB/OBJ × TEO/PRA | STT · STM · SGT · SGE | the four ***adreçadors*** (addressors) — sense, feeling, meaning, sign |

Each cycle visits 4 cardinal axis poles + 4 secondary mediators, forming an 8-point loop. See [`../methodology/metode-global.md`](../methodology/metode-global.md) for the full account of the Mètode Global cycle.

## Application Cycle *(Volta d'Aplicació)* — the Mètode Global

**Trajectory** *(8-point cycle)*: ANA → TEO → SIN → NOU → AMO → PRA → EXP → FEN → (ANA)

Phases at corners; axis poles as bridges between phases. This is the canonical trajectory for *analysis, reasoning, problem-solving, planning* — the form a structured deliberation takes.

| Phase | Position | Coords (y, z) | Bridge to next |
|-------|----------|---------------|----------------|
| **ANA** *(Analysis)* | TEO + FEN | (60, 77) | TEO |
| **SIN** *(Synthesis)* | TEO + NOU | (60, -77) | NOU |
| **AMO** *(Love)* | PRA + NOU | (-60, -77) | PRA |
| **EXP** *(Experience)* | PRA + FEN | (-60, 77) | FEN (closes loop) |

### Detailed descriptions

### **ANA** — Analysis *(CA: Anàlisi)*

**Axis poles**: TEO + FEN · coords (x,y,z) = (0, 60, 77)

Discernment. Mental abstraction. Qualities and accidents. Nuance.

### **SIN** — Synthesis *(CA: Síntesi)*

**Axis poles**: TEO + NOU · coords (x,y,z) = (0, 60, -77)

Wisdom. Abstract unitary grasp of transcendent reality.

### **AMO** — Love *(CA: Amor)*

**Axis poles**: PRA + NOU · coords (x,y,z) = (0, -60, -77)

Good. The full internal freedom of all transcendent concrete reality. Universal practical communion. Concretion of transcendence.

### **EXP** — Experience *(CA: Experiència)*

**Axis poles**: PRA + FEN · coords (x,y,z) = (0, -60, 77)

Situation. Direct observation. Work. Strength and Challenge of each concrete event.

## Revelation Cycle *(Volta de Revelació)* — the four addressors

**Trajectory**: STM → STT → SGT → SGE (then back to STM via the cardinal poles SUB-TEO-OBJ-PRA).

The four *adreçadors* (addressors) are the orientations through which subjective experience and objective reality address each other. This cycle is canonical for *orientation, alignment, conflict resolution, finding direction and meaning* — questions like "What do I feel?", "What do I want?", "What do I commit to?", "What conditions do I need?".

| Addressor | Position | Coords (x, y) | Stage |
|-----------|----------|---------------|-------|
| **STM** *(Feeling)* | SUB + PRA | (-80, -60) | Desires |
| **STT** *(Sense)* | SUB + TEO | (-80, 60) | Aspirations |
| **SGT** *(Meaning)* | OBJ + TEO | (80, 60) | Commitments |
| **SGE** *(Sign)* | OBJ + PRA | (80, -60) | Conditions |

### Detailed descriptions

### **STM** — Feeling *(CA: Sentiment)*

**Axis poles**: SUB + PRA · coords (x,y,z) = (-80, -60, 0)

Soul. Drive and vital force. The permanent emotion of living.

### **STT** — Sense *(CA: Sentit)*

**Axis poles**: SUB + TEO · coords (x,y,z) = (-80, 60, 0)

Intelligence. Guess. Game.

### **SGT** — Meaning *(CA: Significat)*

**Axis poles**: OBJ + TEO · coords (x,y,z) = (80, 60, 0)

Definition. The accepted definition of objects. The scholarly world of memory.

### **SGE** — Sign *(CA: Signe)*

**Axis poles**: OBJ + PRA · coords (x,y,z) = (80, -60, 0)

Things. This. Concrete external or internal signs and warnings.

## Dialectical pairings

Within each volta, the 4 categories form 2 dialectical pairs (opposite corners of the 2D square they trace):

### Application Cycle pairs

| | dialectical opposite | shared axis | difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ANA** *(Analysis)* | ↔ **AMO** *(Love)* | y/z diagonal | OBJ-distinction vs SUB-care; theoretical vs practical |
| **SIN** *(Synthesis)* | ↔ **EXP** *(Experience)* | y/z diagonal | OBJ-articulation vs SUB-living; theoretical vs practical |

### Revelation Cycle pairs

| | dialectical opposite | shared axis | difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| **STM** *(Feeling)* | ↔ **SGT** *(Meaning)* | x/y diagonal | subjective drive vs objective definition |
| **STT** *(Sense)* | ↔ **SGE** *(Sign)* | x/y diagonal | subjective interpretation vs objective signal |
