# The four reflective axes

The Meta-Globàlium organizes reality along **four reflective dialectical axes**. They are *reflective* because they apply to themselves: each axis can be characterized using the others. They are *dialectical* because each is a polarity, not a category — a tension that no full account can collapse.

The four axes appear, with different formulations, across major philosophical traditions: German idealism, hermeneutics, European phenomenology, Buddhist mādhyamika, Confucian relational thought. The Meta-Globàlium does not claim to invent them — it claims to **order them geometrically and make them operative**.

## The first three axes (Cartesian: 3 orthogonal directions)

| Axis | Negative pole | Positive pole | Tension |
|------|---------------|---------------|---------|
| **D1** | **OBJ** — *objective* | **SUB** — *subjective* | The given world vs. the experiencing self |
| **D2** | **TEO** — *theory* | **PRA** — *practice* | Reflection on what is vs. action that changes it |
| **D3** | **NOU** — *noumenon* | **FEN** — *phenomenon* | What is in itself vs. what appears |

These three axes generate the Cartesian skeleton of the model. Combinations of poles produce the 8 root categories (the corners of the 3D cube).

## The fourth axis (radial: tempeternitat)

| Axis | Inner pole | Outer pole | Tension |
|------|-----------|-----------|---------|
| **D4** | **PLA** — *plasma* | **MON** — *world* | Atemporal foundation vs. temporal manifestation |

D4 is **radial**, not Cartesian. It is the *meta-dimensional* axis: it operates on every other pole, generating three layers of expression (Plasmàtica / Neutral / Mundana) for each cardinal direction.

Geometrically: D4 is the radius from the center of the hypersphere outward. PLA is the center (zero radius); MON is the surface (full radius); the Neutral sphere is the mediator at intermediate radius.

Conceptually, D4 is **tempeternitat** — *time + eternity* — a category that does not reduce to either pole alone. The plasma is not "before" the world; the world is not "after" the plasma. They are the same reality at different radii of expression.

## Why "reflective"

Each axis can be expressed *through* the others:

- *Theory* (D2-) can be done *objectively* (D1-) or *subjectively* (D1+).
- *Subject* (D1+) emerges in the *phenomenon* (D3+) but its essence belongs to the *noumenon* (D3-).
- The *world* (D4+) is the manifestation through which the *plasma* (D4-) becomes accessible.

This reflective structure is what allows the model to be **fractal**: the same axes can be re-applied at any scale or thematic frame (see `fractal-N1-N2.md`).

## The axes in mathematical operations

| Axis | Operation pair |
|------|----------------|
| **D1 (OBJ/SUB)** | identity ↔ polarity |
| **D2 (TEO/PRA)** | distinction ↔ correspondence |
| **D3 (NOU/FEN)** | similarity ↔ divergence |
| **D4 (PLA/MON)** | convergence ↔ difference |

These eight operations are the mathematical primitives the Meta-Globàlium claims as the geometry of any unified reasoning. See [canonical-mappings.md](../methodology/canonical-mappings.md) for cross-system correspondences (Aristotelian categories, logic gates, alchemical principles).

## Axes and the four phases of the Mètode Global

The four phases of the global method (ANA → SIN → AMO → EXP) map directly to the four axes:

| Phase | Axis | Function |
|-------|------|----------|
| **ANA** (analysis) | D3- (NOU) | Distinction of parts |
| **SIN** (synthesis) | D2- (TEO) | Determination of relations |
| **AMO** (love) | D1+ (SUB) | Plan of action through care |
| **EXP** (experience) | D2+ (PRA) | Implementation in the world |

This mapping is what allows the verifier to be **process-aware**: not "did the answer cover all axes?" but "did the reasoning move through the four phases coherently?".
