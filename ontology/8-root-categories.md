# The 8 root categories

The 8 root categories are the **corners of the 3D Cartesian skeleton** generated by the first three axes (D1: OBJ/SUB · D2: TEO/PRA · D3: NOU/FEN). Each root category is a unique combination of one pole from each axis.

| # | Code | OBJ/SUB | TEO/PRA | NOU/FEN | Description |
|---|------|---------|---------|---------|-------------|
| 1 | **ANA** | OBJ | TEO | NOU | Analysis — distinction of parts |
| 2 | **SIN** | OBJ | TEO | FEN | Synthesis — relations among parts |
| 3 | **AMO** | SUB | PRA | NOU | Love — plan of action with care |
| 4 | **EXP** | SUB | PRA | FEN | Experience — implementation in the world |
| 5 | **LOG** | OBJ | PRA | NOU | Logic — practical objectivity |
| 6 | **MIS** | SUB | TEO | FEN | Mystery — subjective theorisation of the phenomenal |
| 7 | **TEC** | OBJ | PRA | FEN | Technique — phenomenal-objective practice |
| 8 | **MIT** | SUB | TEO | NOU | Myth — subjective noumenal theorisation |

These 8 are the **operational primitives** of the model. The full 80 categories are layered expressions (Plasmàtica / Neutral / Mundana) of these root combinations.
