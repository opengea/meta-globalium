# Fractal architecture — N1 universal principles + N2 thematized expressions

The Meta-Globàlium is **fractal**: the same structural primitives (the four reflective axes) project onto every domain as **thematized expressions**. The model is therefore not just an ontology of 80 categories but **a model of models** — a generator of frames.

## Two levels

### N1 — Universal principles

The N1 layer is the structural skeleton: the **4 axes**, the **8 root categories**, and the dialectical operations that combine them. N1 is invariant across domains — it is what makes the geometry universal.

### N2 — Thematized expressions

The N2 layer is the application of N1 to a specific domain. Each thematized frame uses the same axes and same root structure, but with domain-specific labels and content.

For example:

| N1 axis | N2 frame: Epistemology | N2 frame: Politics | N2 frame: Pedagogy |
|---------|------------------------|--------------------|--------------------|
| OBJ ↔ SUB | data ↔ interpretation | structure ↔ agency | content ↔ learner |
| TEO ↔ PRA | hypothesis ↔ experiment | principle ↔ governance | theory ↔ practicum |
| NOU ↔ FEN | essence ↔ phenomenon | constitution ↔ election | concept ↔ skill |
| PLA ↔ MON | foundation ↔ application | tradition ↔ situation | discipline ↔ context |

Every N2 frame is a **80-category map of its domain**, structurally homologous to the canonical 80.

## Why this matters

If the hypothesis holds, the same primitives project onto:
- Disciplines (epistemology, politics, ethics, pedagogy, ecology, economics…)
- Practices (medicine, law, engineering, design, governance…)
- Decisions (any concrete decision in any domain)

This is the **fractal claim**: the geometry that situates the totality of human reality replicates itself, preserving its form, over every discipline, practice, or decision.

If true, **interdisciplinarity rests on a common geometry** rather than ad-hoc translation. A philosopher and an engineer working on the same problem can map their respective concerns onto the same N1 structure and find the actual disagreements (which often turn out to be on different axes than expected).

## The math

If N1 has 80 categories, and a frame is a thematization of N1, then **every domain has its own 80 categories**. With 80 frames currently identified, this generates:

> **80 × 80 = 6 400 derived metacategories**

This is what the database calls "metacategories" (`kms_kb_metacategories` — 3103 active, with more in development).

## How to create a new frame (N2)

The protocol is strict by design. See [`../methodology/protocol-N2.md`](../methodology/protocol-N2.md). Summary:

1. Identify the domain
2. Map N1 axes to domain-specific polarities
3. Validate dialectical coherence (not just word substitution)
4. Cross-check against existing frames for consistency
5. Provide both Card A (source attribution) and Card B (Meta-Globàlium structural mapping)
6. Submit for community review

Without this protocol, frames trivially proliferate but lose coherence.
