# The 12 discipline categories

Twelve categories of the Neutral sphere (generation 1.75) correspond to the **academic disciplines** as articulated by the Meta-Globàlium. They occupy the ridges and intermediate positions between cardinal axis poles, providing a structural map of how human knowledge organizes itself disciplinarily.

These are not arbitrary labels imposed on contemporary academic departments — they emerge from the dialectical tensions between the four reflective axes (see [`4-axes.md`](4-axes.md)) and the 8 root poles (see [`8-root-categories.md`](8-root-categories.md)).

## Detailed descriptions

### **CIE** — Science *(CA: Ciència)* · coords (x,y,z) = (70, 0, 70)

Knowledge and treatment of physical reality formally and experimentally rigorous. Exact knowledge and prediction of the order of things. Objective treatment of changing reality (phenomena). Knowledge. Recognition. Expert. Enlightenment. Identification. Verification/falsification. Testing. Experimental science. Size. Measure. Metrics. Compass. Rhythm. Constants and variables.

### **ART** — Art *(CA: Art)* · coords (x,y,z) = (-70, 0, 70)

Aesthetic rigorous treatment of changing reality (psychic, interior, and experiential). Creative expression. Reverie. Creation. Poetry. Touch. Precision. Clinical eye. Alteration. Dream. Not just the fine arts, but also: education, medicine, politics, demotics, soul care, judiciary, journalism, etc. Mask. Precision without law. Unconscious mathematics. Asceticism.

### **MTF** — Metaphysics *(CA: Metafísica)* · coords (x,y,z) = (70, 0, -70)

Deep cause and essence of everything. What we believe beings are. The plus that the whole adds to the sum of the parts. Essence. Faith in the reality of things. Believing that things are. Ontology. The being of beings. Particular, singular reality. Individual substance. The quid of things. Species. Individual. Totalization of a complex. Thing in itself. Realism.

### **MTP** — Metapsychics *(CA: Metapsíquica)* · coords (x,y,z) = (-70, 0, -70)

The mysterious world of the Spirit and spirits. Fecundation between spirits. Spirit. Soul. Animism. Consciousness. Subconscious. Superconscious. Transconscious. Ecstasy. Meditation. Dhyana (Raja-Yoga). Enlightenment. Proper name. Freedom of spirit. Holistic (Ken Wilber). Immortality. My (our) soul. Twin souls. My (our) spirit. Concord. My (our) conscience. Hope. Charisma.

### **MIS** — Mysticism *(CA: Mística)* · coords (x,y,z) = (-55, -60, -55)

Spiritual experience of transcendent reality. Subjective love. Ecstasy. Mystery. Contemplation. Dionysian impulse. Initiatory mysteries. Unspeakability. Ineffability. Sacredness. Mystagogies. Parapsychology. Witchcraft. Witchery. Night of the spirit. Transforming union. Spiritual betrothal and marriage. Divine or diabolical possession.

### **PSI** — Psychic *(CA: Psíquica)* · coords (x,y,z) = (-55, -60, 55)

The intimate experience of everyday reality. Games of sets of inner experiences. Sensitivity. Experiences. Intuitions. Affections. Sensations. Volitions. Perceptions. Images. Thoughts. Feelings. Decisions. Actions. Memories. Education. Introspection. Play. Temperance / debauchery. Real, novelistic, cinematic dramas. Please. Interiority. Intimacy.

### **TEC** — Technique *(CA: Tècnica)* · coords (x,y,z) = (55, -60, 55)

Procedure. Tool. Conditions of use of tools. Professional skill. Efficiency. Laws of sets of physical utility. Physics. Rule. Method. Machine. Tool. Experiment. Repetition. Protocol. Pragmatism. Realism. Tricks. Stratagems. Craftsmanship. Trade. Industry. Disciplines. Computing. Electronics.

### **ETI** — Ethics *(CA: Ètica)* · coords (x,y,z) = (55, -60, -55)

Common good. Transcendence that challenges the vital attitude. Objective love. Rights and duties. Need. Justice. Natural law.

### **MIT** — Mythic *(CA: Mítica)* · coords (x,y,z) = (-55, 60, -55)

Expression or free spiritual delirium. Transcendent narrative. Voice of the soul of the universe. Delirium. Heroism. Epic. Liturgy. Hierarchy. Celebration. Party. Carnival. Prophecy. The feast of fools. Gift of tongues. The imaginary. Cults. Rites.

### **EST** — Aesthetics *(CA: Estètica)* · coords (x,y,z) = (-55, 60, 55)

Figuration. Fantasy. Illusion. Imagination. Free formal expression of subjective affections. Games of sets of subjective images. Simulacrum. Prejudice. Mirage. Fallacy. Imitation. Fable. Confabulation. Configuration. Intrigue. Association. Copulation. Fiction. Sophism. Equivocation. Mistake. Dissonance. Comedy. Metaphor. Hallucination. Dreams. Lie. Deception. Mental games.

### **IDE** — Idea *(CA: Idèica)* · coords (x,y,z) = (55, 60, -55)

Structured wisdom in ideas. Categorization to understand and guide reality. Values. Concept. Being. Idea. Notion. Thought. General. Genre. Group. Dogma. Thesis / hypothesis (assumption). Conjecture. Opinion - doxa. Theme. Name. Term. Representation. Predicate. Predicable (Porphyry). Postulate. Category. Importance. Relevance. Condition. Futurible. Possibility. Question.

### **LOG** — Logic *(CA: Lògica)* · coords (x,y,z) = (55, 60, 55)

Rules of reason. Deduction. Mathematics. Non-contradictory formal expression. Mathematics. Formula. Calculation. Quantity. Algorithm. Induction. Deduction. Derivation. Inference. Equivalence. Equality. Isomorphism. Ideal speech or language.
