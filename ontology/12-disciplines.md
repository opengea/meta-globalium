# The 12 discipline categories

Twelve categories of the Neutral sphere (generation 1.75) correspond to the **academic disciplines** as articulated by the Meta-Globàlium. They occupy positions on the Neutral mediator sphere, providing a structural map of how human knowledge organizes itself disciplinarily.

These are not arbitrary labels imposed on contemporary academic departments — they emerge from the dialectical tensions between the four reflective axes (see [`4-axes.md`](4-axes.md)) and the 8 root poles (see [`8-root-categories.md`](8-root-categories.md)).

## Geometric structure: three groups of four

The 12 disciplines split into **three groups of four**, by their position along the D2 axis (TEO ↔ PRA, the y-coordinate):

| Group | y-coord | Side | Disciplines | Common property |
|-------|---------|------|-------------|------------------|
| **A — Equatorial** | y = 0 | between TEO and PRA | CIE · ART · MTF · MTP | direct treatment of reality, neither purely theoretical nor purely practical |
| **B — Practical hemisphere** | y = -60 | PRA side | TEC · ETI · MIS · PSI | disciplines of action, intervention, and lived experience |
| **C — Theoretical hemisphere** | y = +60 | TEO side | LOG · IDE · MIT · EST | disciplines of formal articulation, ideation, and theoretical expression |

Within each group, the four disciplines are positioned by combining one pole each from D1 (OBJ/SUB) and D3 (NOU/FEN), forming a 4-corner 2D square in their respective plane.

## Group A — Equatorial disciplines *(y = 0)*

Four disciplines occupy the equatorial plane between the theoretical (TEO) and practical (PRA) hemispheres. They treat reality directly, combining objectivity/subjectivity with phenomenon/noumenon.

### **ART** — Art *(CA: Art)*

**Axis poles**: SUB + FEN · coords (x,y,z) = (-70, 0, 70)

Aesthetic rigorous treatment of changing reality (psychic, interior, and experiential). Creative expression. Reverie. Creation. Poetry. Touch. Precision. Clinical eye. Alteration. Dream. Not just the fine arts, but also: education, medicine, politics, demotics, soul care, judiciary, journalism, etc. Mask. Precision without law. Unconscious mathematics. Asceticism.

### **CIE** — Science *(CA: Ciència)*

**Axis poles**: OBJ + FEN · coords (x,y,z) = (70, 0, 70)

Knowledge and treatment of physical reality formally and experimentally rigorous. Exact knowledge and prediction of the order of things. Objective treatment of changing reality (phenomena). Knowledge. Recognition. Expert. Enlightenment. Identification. Verification/falsification. Testing. Experimental science. Size. Measure. Metrics. Compass. Rhythm. Constants and variables. Magnitude.

### **MTF** — Metaphysics *(CA: Metafísica)*

**Axis poles**: OBJ + NOU · coords (x,y,z) = (70, 0, -70)

Deep cause and essence of everything. What we believe beings are. The plus that the whole adds to the sum of the parts. Essence. Faith in the reality of things. Believing that things are. Ontology. The being of beings. Particular, singular reality. Individual substance. The quid of things. Species. Individual. Totalization of a complex. Thing in itself. Realism. Cybernetic balance. Resting mass.

### **MTP** — Metapsychics *(CA: Metapsíquica)*

**Axis poles**: SUB + NOU · coords (x,y,z) = (-70, 0, -70)

The mysterious world of the Spirit and spirits. Fecundation between spirits. Spirit. Soul. Animism. Consciousness. Subconscious. Superconscious. Transconscious. Ecstasy. Meditation. Dhyana (Raja-Yoga). Enlightenment. Proper name. Freedom of spirit. Holistic (Ken Wilber). Immortality. My (our) soul. Twin souls. My (our) spirit. Concord. My (our) conscience. Hope. Charisma. Vocation. Call.

## Group B — Practical hemisphere *(y = -60, PRA side)*

Four disciplines on the practical hemisphere — the side of action, intervention, and lived experience.

### **ETI** — Ethics *(CA: Ètica)*

**Axis poles**: OBJ + PRA + NOU · coords (x,y,z) = (55, -60, -55)

Common good. Transcendence that challenges the vital attitude. Objective love. Rights and duties. Need. Justice. Natural law.

### **MIS** — Mysticism *(CA: Mística)*

**Axis poles**: SUB + PRA + NOU · coords (x,y,z) = (-55, -60, -55)

Spiritual experience of transcendent reality. Subjective love. Ecstasy. Mystery. Contemplation. Dionysian impulse. Initiatory mysteries. Unspeakability. Ineffability. Sacredness. Mystagogies. Parapsychology. Witchcraft. Witchery. Night of the spirit. Transforming union. Spiritual betrothal and marriage. Divine or diabolical possession.

### **PSI** — Psychic *(CA: Psíquica)*

**Axis poles**: SUB + PRA + FEN · coords (x,y,z) = (-55, -60, 55)

The intimate experience of everyday reality. Games of sets of inner experiences. Sensitivity. Experiences. Intuitions. Affections. Sensations. Volitions. Perceptions. Images. Thoughts. Feelings. Decisions. Actions. Memories. Education. Introspection. Play. Temperance / debauchery. Real, novelistic, cinematic dramas. Please. Interiority. Intimacy.

### **TEC** — Technique *(CA: Tècnica)*

**Axis poles**: OBJ + PRA + FEN · coords (x,y,z) = (55, -60, 55)

Procedure. Tool. Conditions of use of tools. Professional skill. Efficiency. Laws of sets of physical utility. Physics. Rule. Method. Machine. Tool. Experiment. Repetition. Protocol. Pragmatism. Realism. Tricks. Stratagems. Craftsmanship. Trade. Industry. Disciplines. Computing. Electronics.

## Group C — Theoretical hemisphere *(y = +60, TEO side)*

Four disciplines on the theoretical hemisphere — the side of formal articulation, ideation, and abstract expression.

### **EST** — Aesthetics *(CA: Estètica)*

**Axis poles**: SUB + TEO + FEN · coords (x,y,z) = (-55, 60, 55)

Figuration. Fantasy. Illusion. Imagination. Free formal expression of subjective affections. Games of sets of subjective images. Simulacrum. Prejudice. Mirage. Fallacy. Imitation. Fable. Confabulation. Configuration. Intrigue. Association. Copulation. Fiction. Sophism. Equivocation. Mistake. Dissonance. Comedy. Metaphor. Hallucination. Dreams. Lie. Deception. Mental games. Jokes. Riddles.

### **IDE** — Idea *(CA: Idèica)*

**Axis poles**: OBJ + TEO + NOU · coords (x,y,z) = (55, 60, -55)

Structured wisdom in ideas. Categorization to understand and guide reality. Values. Concept. Being. Idea. Notion. Thought. General. Genre. Group. Dogma. Thesis / hypothesis (assumption). Conjecture. Opinion - doxa. Theme. Name. Term. Representation. Predicate. Predicable (Porphyry). Postulate. Category. Importance. Relevance. Condition. Futurible. Possibility. Question. Purpose. Reason.

### **LOG** — Logic *(CA: Lògica)*

**Axis poles**: OBJ + TEO + FEN · coords (x,y,z) = (55, 60, 55)

Rules of reason. Deduction. Mathematics. Non-contradictory formal expression. Mathematics. Formula. Calculation. Quantity. Algorithm. Induction. Deduction. Derivation. Inference. Equivalence. Equality. Isomorphism. Ideal speech or language.

### **MIT** — Mythic *(CA: Mítica)*

## Dialectical pairings

Across hemispheres, each practical discipline has its theoretical counterpart at the same (x, z) coordinates:

| Practical (B) | ↔ | Theoretical (C) | Axis pair |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TEC** *(Technique)* | ↔ | **LOG** *(Logic)* | OBJ + FEN |
| **ETI** *(Ethics)* | ↔ | **IDE** *(Idea)* | OBJ + NOU |
| **PSI** *(Psychic)* | ↔ | **EST** *(Aesthetics)* | SUB + FEN |
| **MIS** *(Mysticism)* | ↔ | **MIT** *(Mythic)* | SUB + NOU |

And in the equatorial group, the 4 disciplines pair across the OBJ-SUB and FEN-NOU axes:

| | OBJ side | SUB side |
|---|----------|----------|
| **FEN** (phenomenon) | **CIE** *(Science)* | **ART** *(Art)* |
| **NOU** (noumenon) | **MTF** *(Metaphysics)* | **MTP** *(Metapsychics)* |
